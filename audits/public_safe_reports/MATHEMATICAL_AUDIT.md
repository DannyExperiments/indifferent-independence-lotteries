# Mathematical audit

Every generating step increases the vector mean weakly. Equality of the means forces a dominating coupling to be almost surely diagonal because its differences are nonnegative coordinatewise. Thus the convex same-mean families remain indifference classes after adjoining transitivity, and every class outside those families is a singleton.

A strict comparison must have unequal means. Couplings in the outer portions of a comparison chain glue along their shared finite marginals. A positive-weight good component supplies atoms above and below its mean, strictly so for a nontrivial component. If the interior means agree, strictness comes from an outer comparison and the strict atom in the correct support still survives. This handles the boundary case in the Negative Dominance proof.

The theorem uses the positive-mass strict coupling condition in the pinned catalog. Lederman’s generalized lottery definition allows zero weights; those matched atoms are deleted before checking strictness. The lattice replay tests dominated couplings and all represented witnesses in the finite closure. The non-chain cross mixture and incomparable antidiagonal fixture have the same mean.
