When mixtures tie

Every displayed lottery has mean (0, 0). The construction treats a lottery as indifferent to the sure mean when it can be built from good lotteries with that mean.

Lottery

Which same-mean mixtures are allowed?

Each horizontal or vertical outcome pair is comparable coordinatewise, so each of those lotteries is good. Every mixture of them is in the same convex indifference class. The antidiagonal's two outcomes are incomparable. There is no good mean-zero lottery supported on just those two outcomes, so the antidiagonal stays outside the sure mean's class.
